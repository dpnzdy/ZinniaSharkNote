OPTIONAL SUPPORT MATERIALS

Before sending optional materials.

Use this page when general support requires additional materials or when sending feature suggestions.

Even without consent, you can use free document editing and general support. This page does not send files or email to a server. After reviewing, send the email yourself from your email app.

If support has not requested materials, start with a general inquiry. Do not send original documents, passwords or sensitive information.

1. Collection and use consent

Information: reply email address and the suggestion you provide. Purpose: reviewing and replying to suggestions. Retention: within 1 year after inquiry closure, with earlier deletion when no longer needed or consent is withdrawn, unless legally required to retain it.

2. Separate consent to overseas transfer

Recipient: ZOHO Corporation Pte Ltd (Singapore; privacy@zohocorp.com). Mail is stored and operated in the United States through Zoho Technologies. Zoho Corporation Pvt. Ltd. in India may access it as necessary during technical support. The contracting entity’s Singapore location does not mean mail is stored in Singapore.

Information transferred: reply address, sender display name, email transmission information and consented materials. Purposes: receiving, storing and replying to inquiries and handling the relevant diagnostics or suggestions. Timing/method: network transmission, storage and access when emails are sent/replied to and during necessary service operations. Operational retention matches the collection/use periods above. After trash deletion, Zoho server recovery copies may remain for an additional 30 days.

If you refuse, you may refrain from sending optional materials through this channel. Local editing and general inquiries remain available. Request withdrawal or suspension at the contact email address.
