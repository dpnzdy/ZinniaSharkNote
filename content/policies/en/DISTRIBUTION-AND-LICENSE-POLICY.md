## 1. Windows Free and Pro

Windows Free and Pro are installed as separate Microsoft Store apps. Free is free of charge and allows adding up to 500 top-level blocks and 15 media items per document. Pro offers a 30-day free trial and a USD $15.99 one-time purchase. Check each Store product page for regional prices, trial acquisition and purchase conditions.

Pro's block/media count limits are removed during the trial and after purchase. Technical limits such as file size, structure and memory still apply. Free may be used for personal, educational, business and commercial purposes.

The current Windows app saves to .adq files with Ctrl+S and offers Save, Discard or Cancel when closing. It does not provide session resumption or periodic recovery-copy recording. If older working data or recovery copies remain, use File → Export documents from previous versions to save separate .adq files.

See [Downloads and edition comparison](download.html#editions) for current products and installation conditions. These are distinguished from the features and distribution history of the earlier public 1.0 edition in [Release news](releases.html#release-history).

## 2. Platform structure

| Platform | Availability | Pro purchase method |
|---|---|---|
| Windows | Free and Pro installed as separate Microsoft Store apps | 30-day free trial and one-time purchase |
| Android | Google Play public release being prepared; purchase conversion within one app planned | One-time purchase, monthly subscription and annual subscription planned |

Windows and Android purchase rights are acquired separately by platform. Supported .adq documents saved in Windows Free can be opened in Pro. Free and Pro manage app settings and older-version data in their respective apps.

Android's release date, pricing, trial and purchase integration are separate development plans. Current Windows trials and prices do not apply to Android.

## 3. Document editing in Free and Pro

Windows Free allows adding up to 500 top-level blocks and 15 media items per document. The Android development edition uses the same count limits. Each top-level block, such as a table, list or quotation, counts as one; media items inside table cells and elsewhere are included in the media count.

Existing supported documents over the limits can be opened and saved while preserving content and media. Editing that does not increase an already over-limit item count is also permitted. Pro removes block and media count limits. Technical limits based on file size, structure and runtime environment apply to both editions. Both use the common ADQ2/logical v3 format.

See the [User guide](guide.html#save) for current Windows saving, and [Windows and Android news](development.html) for Android development status and technical media limits. Save documents to .adq files before the Pro trial ends. Pro may be restricted from launching after the Store's time-limited trial expires.

## 4. Purchase rights and updates

A one-time purchase grants use of the purchased major version's Pro features without a time limit. You retain rights to those features after updates or a new major release. Purchase terms for new Pro features in future major versions are announced before sale.

Android monthly/annual subscriptions are being prepared to provide the latest Pro features during their validity. Expiration of a subscription does not remove Pro features of a major version separately bought through a one-time purchase.

A perpetual license covers the features of the purchased major version. See the [Support Lifecycle and Updates Policy](SUPPORT-LIFECYCLE-AND-UPDATES.md) and version-specific notices for support periods and operating system compatibility.
