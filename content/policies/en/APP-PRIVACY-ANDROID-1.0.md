## 2. On-device processing and your choices

### Google Play purchases and restoration

Google Play handles purchases and subscription payments. Through Play billing services, the app receives product IDs, purchase status, signed purchase records and purchase tokens, checks entitlements on the device and acknowledges purchases to Google Play. Payment card or bank account information is not entered in the app. Signed purchase records for permanent entitlements are stored in app-private storage for offline use and removed if a Play query confirms that the purchase is not owned or if app data is deleted. Subscription ownership is checked again during app use. The Google Play billing library includes network components and may communicate with Google for billing service operation and error diagnostics. Purchase tokens and document content are not sent to a separate developer billing server. Review codes are checked on the device, and reviewer access applies only to the current running session.

### 2.1 Documents, media and records

Document content, inserted media and document passwords are used for editing, display, playback and saving on your Android device. The app has no feature that automatically uploads documents to the developer's servers or sends advertising, visitor analytics or automatic error reports. Documents are not collected for advertising or AI training.

| Information processed or stored on the device | Purpose and management |
|---|---|
| ADQ documents and inserted assets saved by you | Editing and preservation; reading and saving files in storage you choose through the system file picker |
| Current document, selection and undo/redo state | Maintaining the current editing session; no new automatic recovery copies are created, and full restoration of unsaved state at the next launch is not guaranteed |
| Original copy before saving | One internal copy to protect the file immediately before writing to the original; replaced at the next original save; view, export or delete through the app's copy storage management |
| Recent/favorite document metadata and file access grants | Reopening documents you selected; clearing lists differs from deleting original files; unnecessary grants are cleaned up according to related records and open document state |
| App settings, including autosave | Maintaining your chosen editing and saving environment |
| Internal media assets and preview cache | Document display, playback and editing history; may remain while referenced by documents, undo history or the clipboard; removed when references are released or caches are cleaned |
| Remaining copies from past development versions | Explicitly opened or deleted by you; existing copies retained until restoration and saving a verified new file are complete |

Internal app information is processed in Android app-private storage. Some content is processed only in memory; internal storage/cache and external ADQ files you save have different lifetimes. The developer has no function for remotely viewing or deleting your documents, records or passwords.

The clipboard is used when you copy, cut or paste. Shared media is processed when you select ZinniaShark Note as a share target in another app or choose files yourself in the file picker. Subsequent processing by the clipboard, other apps or chosen storage is subject to those services and device settings.

### 2.2 Camera and microphone permissions

Camera permission is requested when you choose to capture a photo or short video inside the app. Microphone permission is requested when you choose to include sound in a video. Captures are processed as document assets that you confirm and insert; they are not automatically sent to the developer's servers.

General document editing remains available if you deny permission. Video capture without sound is available without microphone permission. You can change or revoke permissions in Android settings; features for which permission is denied or revoked may be limited.

### 2.3 Saving, protection and deletion

Periodic autosave is off by default. For a new document, you must first choose a file save location. The app also attempts to save when you leave its screen, but saving may not finish because of system suspension, storage errors or pending input confirmation. A forced exit may lose changes made since the last save. The original copy preserved before saving is not a feature for restoring the latest unsaved input.

Protected documents are saved with the password you specify. Internal media assets use the app's encrypted storage path, but not all settings, file names or metadata are encrypted with the document password. Original-change checks, pre-save backups and post-save rereading do not guarantee all aspects of a storage provider's completed save or remote synchronization.

Recent/favorite records and settings can be managed in the app. Uninstalling or resetting app data may remove internal data and file access grants; restoration on reinstallation is not guaranteed. You manage and delete external ADQ documents directly in your chosen storage. Deleting internal app data differs from deleting external documents. Save important content to external files and verify separate copies before uninstalling.

The app is configured to disallow Android automatic backup and device-to-device transfer of app data. This does not delete or stop files you saved in external storage or that provider's backups and synchronization. Operating system, device manufacturer and storage behavior can vary by environment.

### 2.4 External services and support

Choosing cloud storage through the system file picker makes that provider's account, network, synchronization and privacy terms applicable. Rereading a file in the app does not by itself confirm completion of synchronization to a server or another device. Google Play, Android, WebView, external links and email apps may involve communication and information processing by those services. On-device editing does not guarantee that all external components operate without network communication.

You can read this policy offline under app information in settings. The email inquiry feature only opens a mail compose screen; it does not automatically attach or send documents or diagnostic materials. The terms for support inquiries and optional materials you send yourself follow below.

## 5. Retention and deletion

The inquiry closure date is recorded as the day the response and support handling are completed. If handling ends because there is no response, the final closure notice date is recorded after giving notice; closure dates are not arbitrarily extended. Reopening the same issue does not automatically extend the retention deadline for existing diagnostic materials.

When a retention deadline arrives, the inquiry or diagnostic materials are deleted from business storage locations such as the inbox, sent folder and archive, and also removed from the trash. Diagnostic materials are kept separate from general inquiries and deleted on the 90-day basis. Business copies downloaded by the handler are removed by the same deadline. Quotes and reattachments in other emails are also checked to avoid leaving materials subject to deletion behind.

The mailbox currently automatically cleans spam and trash every 30 days. This cycle is not used to extend retention: due materials are also manually removed from the trash. Zoho Mail may retain emails removed from the trash for an additional 30 days for server recovery. Business deletion is therefore not described as immediate permanent deletion of every server copy. Recovery copies are not reused for general support or other purposes. See the [Zoho Mail data deletion policy](https://www.zoho.com/mail/help/data-deletion-policy.html) for details.

The provider does not operate separate mail-client retrieval, automatic forwarding, mail exports or external mail backups. Temporary diagnostic copies needed for work are deleted within the above periods and are distinguished from the mail service provider's recovery copies. The current Mail Lite service's default eDiscovery/legal long-term retention features are not included in the scope of use.

If separate retention is actually required by law, the applicable law, records and period are checked individually; the records are stored separately and used only for that purpose. No blanket long-term retention exception without a legal basis is applied. Electronic materials are removed using the service's deletion function, and any printed materials are shredded.

## 6. Access restrictions and safeguards

Business handling of support inquiries and diagnostic materials is restricted to the privacy officer. Inquiry materials are not moved to personal messengers or public repositories, and original documents or passwords are not requested. Optional diagnostic materials are handled after checking the requested scope and consent; unnecessary attachments are removed early.

Case management records contain only minimum information such as case number, receipt/closure dates, consent confirmation date, scheduled deletion date and deletion confirmation date. Inquiry bodies, email addresses and original attachments are not duplicated in those records. Temporary business copies are not shared with others and follow the same deletion rules as email.

Minimum management records are deleted 30 days after the last business deletion confirmation, when the recovery-check purpose ends. Records requiring separate statutory retention are separated only for the applicable scope and period.
