## 2. Local document processing in the app

Document content, inserted media and document passwords are used for editing and saving on your PC. The app has no feature that automatically uploads documents to the provider's servers or sends advertising, visitor analytics or automatic error reports. Documents are not collected for advertising or AI training.

| Information stored on the PC | Purpose and management |
|---|---|
| ADQ documents and inserted assets saved by you | Editing and preservation; managed directly at the path you choose |
| Open documents, unsaved content, original paths and working state | Resuming work at the next launch; removed from the resume list when a tab finishes closing; old internal data cleaned up after successful preservation |
| Original copy preserved before saving | Protecting the original against file saving errors |
| Recent document names and paths | Reopening the last 10 documents; clearing the list removes records, not the original documents |
| Settings, toolbar, shortcuts, outline width and recent special characters | Preserving your editing environment |
| Temporary media data | Display and playback; removed during normal cleanup, but some may remain after an abnormal exit |

This local information is separate from support inquiry information received remotely by the provider. The provider does not remotely view or delete documents or records on your PC. Internal working data in the Store-installed edition may be deleted by uninstalling or resetting the app and may not return on reinstallation. Save necessary unsaved work to external ADQ files and make separate backups before uninstalling.

Working data for password-protected documents uses the document's protection settings, and some metadata uses Windows user protection. Not all local information is encrypted with the document password, and changing a password does not retroactively encrypt earlier plaintext copies.

Opening external links or using Microsoft Store, Windows or WebView2 may involve communication and information processing by those services. The app's local operation does not guarantee that all external components operate without network communication.

Inquiries are received when you send them yourself by email. The app does not automatically transmit documents or diagnostic materials.

Your name, telephone number, address, original document and document password are not mandatory for general inquiries. Do not send sensitive information, unique identification information or other people's personal information.

Before sending optional diagnostic materials or suggestions unnecessary for general support, review the information, purposes, periods and overseas transfers in the [Optional Materials Consent](support-consent.html) page and separately agree to collection/use and overseas transfer. Send only the minimum diagnostic materials requested by the person handling your case, in a separate email; do not repeatedly attach them to general inquiry emails. You may refuse to provide optional materials or consent and still use free document editing and general support. However, a particular problem may be difficult to reproduce without additional materials. You can request withdrawal of consent at the contact email address. Materials unrelated to support, or unnecessary materials sent without consent, are deleted after minimum review and are not used for further analysis.

## 5. Retention and deletion

The inquiry closure date is recorded as the day the response and support handling are completed. If handling ends because there is no response, the final closure notice date is recorded after giving notice; closure dates are not arbitrarily extended. Reopening the same issue does not automatically extend the retention deadline for existing diagnostic materials.

When a retention deadline arrives, the inquiry or diagnostic materials are deleted from business storage locations such as the inbox, sent folder and archive, and also removed from the trash. Diagnostic materials are kept separate from general inquiries and deleted on the 90-day basis. Business copies downloaded by the handler are removed by the same deadline. Quotes and reattachments in other emails are also checked to avoid leaving materials subject to deletion behind.

The mailbox currently automatically cleans spam and trash every 30 days. This cycle is not used to extend retention: due materials are also manually removed from the trash. Zoho Mail may retain emails removed from the trash for an additional 30 days for server recovery. Business deletion is therefore not described as immediate permanent deletion of every server copy. Recovery copies are not reused for general support or other purposes. See the [Zoho Mail data deletion policy](https://www.zoho.com/mail/help/data-deletion-policy.html) for details.

The provider does not operate separate mail-client retrieval, automatic forwarding, mail exports or external mail backups. Temporary diagnostic copies needed for work are deleted within the above periods and are distinguished from the mail service provider's recovery copies. The current Mail Lite service's default eDiscovery/legal long-term retention features are not included in the scope of use.

If separate retention is actually required by law, the applicable law, records and period are checked individually; the records are stored separately and used only for that purpose. No blanket long-term retention exception without a legal basis is applied. Electronic materials are removed using the service's deletion function, and any printed materials are shredded.

## 6. Access restrictions and safeguards

Business handling of support inquiries and diagnostic materials is restricted to the privacy officer. Inquiry materials are not moved to personal messengers or public repositories, and original documents or passwords are not requested. Optional diagnostic materials are handled after checking the requested scope and consent; unnecessary attachments are removed early.

Case management records contain only minimum information such as case number, receipt/closure dates, consent confirmation date, scheduled deletion date and deletion confirmation date. Inquiry bodies, email addresses and original attachments are not duplicated in those records. Temporary business copies are not shared with others and follow the same deletion rules as email.

Minimum management records are deleted 30 days after the last business deletion confirmation, when the recovery-check purpose ends. Records requiring separate statutory retention are separated only for the applicable scope and period.

## 7. Rights requests and children's information

You may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing or withdrawal of consent for your information at dpnzdy@zinniashark.com. After minimum necessary identity verification, requests are handled within the time limits and procedures prescribed by applicable law. If a restriction applies, the reason and how to object are explained. Lawful authorization is verified for requests made by representatives. You manage local documents on your PC yourself; the provider cannot delete them remotely.

If optional materials from a child under 14 must be collected on the basis of consent, the legal guardian consent and verification process is completed first. Optional materials are not requested before the necessary verification. Free local editing does not require an app account or personal information registration.

For privacy infringement counseling and dispute mediation, you may also consult the [Korean Privacy Portal](https://www.privacy.go.kr/).
